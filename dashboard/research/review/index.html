Continue with your own keys

From a verified source checkout, inspect the downloaded packet, then submit with your existing participant home and its verified client. Replace the two absolute paths below with your own. The client returns its receipt in the packet; import that same file here.

python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py inspect --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json
python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py submit --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py
python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py refresh --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py

After a started or uncertain submission, use refresh first. If the packet remains started without a receipt, deliberately resume through the existing client’s recovery guards:

python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py resume --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py

Do not submit the packet again. Reviewers choose and sign their own reasons and verdicts in their clients. This page never holds a key or signs a transaction.

What this view can tell you

The cutoff applies to contributions, links, concerns, assessments and version references before they are sorted or drawn. An earlier source discovered later stays absent from earlier snapshots. Unknown occurrence dates stay unknown. “Full collection” means this imported export, not everything that exists.

Possible reassessment follows supports from evidence to claim, requires from prerequisite to dependent, and uses-input from input to the activity using it. Shared input is labelled separately.
