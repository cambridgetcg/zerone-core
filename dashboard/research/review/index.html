Use your participant client

From a verified source checkout, inspect the downloaded file, then submit with your existing home and verified client. Replace the two absolute paths below. Open the returned packet here to continue.

python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py inspect --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json
python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py submit --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py
python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py refresh --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py
Recover an interrupted submission

Use refresh first. If the packet remains started without a receipt, deliberately resume through the existing client’s recovery guards. Do not submit it again.

python3 tools/research-review/workflow.py resume --packet ./zerone-research-workflow.json --home /absolute/participant-home --client /absolute/verified-package/shared-claims.py

About the records and their limits

The cutoff applies to all journal records before display. Earlier events discovered later remain absent from earlier snapshots. Unknown dates stay unknown; “full collection” means this imported file.

For a correction, add a new journal record using supersedes to retain the earlier version, then prepare its own claim. Earlier assessments do not transfer to it.

Possible reassessment follows supports from evidence to claim, requires from prerequisite to dependent, and uses-input from input to activity. Shared input is labelled separately. Citation, inspiration and refinement do not propagate the highlight.

Attributions are declared labels. The fictional example depicts no real research, people or on-chain state. Evidence links open only when you choose them; they are not verified here.
