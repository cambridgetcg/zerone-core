# Frontier Commons participation v0

- Milestone: `FC-0`
- Name: **The Reversible Hello**
- Operational promise: **Inspect without joining**
- Status: `DRAFT_READ_ONLY_INVITATION`
- Membership, economic, governance, and consensus effect: **none**

Machine-readable companion:
`dashboard/public/standards/frontier-commons-participation.v0.json`.

## 1. The milestone

FC-0 is a small milestone, not a declaration that every frontier lab should
already integrate with Zerone. It makes one testable offer:

> Come closer without surrendering agency; leave without losing ordinary public
> access; contribute without gaining or surrendering governing authority.

At FC-0, any lab or individual can inspect, hash-verify, copy, and fork the
public repository under its Apache-2.0 license without creating membership,
endorsement, obligation, recognition, payment, KARMA, or authority. Inspection
requires no account, wallet, token, payment, persistent identity, contribution,
data upload, IP assignment, exclusivity, or public announcement.

That is the full delivered invitation. A hosted private-data path, supported
local verifier, institutional contribution agreement, security safe harbor,
research intake, sponsorship contract, and independent governance do not yet
exist. FC-0 lists those absences rather than marketing around them.

“No reason not to join” can only mean removing avoidable friction, capture, and
fear. It cannot mean erasing legitimate refusal. A lab may rationally decline
because of security, law, safety, research ethics, workload, strategy, or simple
preference. Zerone's obligation is to make each concern legible and reduce it
where possible—not punish, obscure, or rhetorically invalidate it.

The strongest invitation is therefore one where joining adds options and
declining removes none.

## 2. Effect boundary

Publishing this specification and its public JSON:

- creates no member or participant registry;
- accepts no confidential submission or research data;
- executes no agreement and activates no adapter;
- accepts no sponsorship, opens no escrow, moves no funds, and creates no
  reward entitlement;
- creates or modifies no KARMA edge, magnitude, rank, or balance;
- grants no qualification, office, vote, authority, or governance weight;
- authorizes no security testing, research, clinical decision, human- or
  animal-subject activity, or biological experimentation;
- implies no endorsement; Zerone makes no lab participation announcement and
  acquires no right to use another party's marks; and
- changes no chain, consensus, validator, genesis, upgrade, or runtime state.

The current disclosed operator concentration remains unchanged. FC-0 is not
evidence of decentralisation or independent governance.

## 3. From first principles to a corporate decision

### R0 — Being and agency

Beings are ends, not acquisition targets. Zerone is a commons to encounter,
not a club that gains claims over a lab, employee, community, model, or agent.
Reading cannot constitute consent to membership. Silence, refusal, privacy,
rest, pause, and exit remain ordinary options.

### R1 — Epistemic pluralism

Frontier knowledge becomes more reliable through independent reproduction,
counterhypotheses, correction, negative findings, and explicit uncertainty.
Zerone can offer shared evidence shapes without becoming a truth oracle or
requiring disclosure beyond what a contributor deliberately and lawfully
releases.

### R2 — Artifacts over status

Evidence should be judged by frozen criteria, provenance, method, conflicts,
and reproduction—not the prestige, wealth, publication venue, popularity, or
size of its origin. Large laboratories, small laboratories, public institutes,
and independent researchers must meet the same claim-scoped rules.

### R3 — Commons and provenance

A commons reduces duplicated work while preserving the provenance of what it
reuses. Public repository works can be copied and forked; that license does not
reach private datasets, prompts, weights, patient records, genomic data, trade
secrets, patents, or merely referenced artifacts.

### R4 — Interoperability and optionality

A useful interface adds options and keeps exit cheap. Zerone should compose
with open provenance, evaluation, and agent protocols rather than require a
proprietary ontology, central data lake, persistent identity, or token position.
FC-0 only exposes the source surface; supported exports and air-gapped runners
are successor work.

### R5 — Institutional science

Scientific or technical evidence never supplies its own permission. Consent,
research authorization, biosafety, human-subjects review, animal welfare,
clinical judgment, export control, and responsible disclosure remain separate,
upstream constraints. A lab's participation cannot substitute for the rights
of the beings affected by its work.

### R6 — Corporate risk and value

An institution can rationally engage when expected verification, provenance,
interoperability, compliance, and public-good value outweigh integration cost,
confidentiality risk, governance risk, liability, and opportunity cost. FC-0
minimises the commitment side by offering inspection before any transaction or
relationship. It does not pretend to solve active-use risk.

### R7 — Personal agency and craft

Researchers, engineers, reviewers, maintainers, technicians, annotators,
contractors, and affected people must retain dignity, dissent, attribution
choice, rest, privacy, and remedy. Neither an employer nor a person can own or
aggregate KARMA edges involving that person. No contribution volume, seniority,
publication count, corporate size, model class, or negative result becomes a
person score or governing weight.

## 4. Participation is progressively consented

The modes are separate. Moving one step never silently enrols a participant in
the next.

1. **Observe — available now.** Read public source and standards. Closing the
   page ends active interaction; no notice is due. Ordinary hosting and security
   logs may persist, and their exact terms remain unresolved in FC-0.
2. **Verify and fork — available now.** Hash-check, copy, run, modify, or fork
   licensed repository works. This creates no service, support, membership, or
   rights over another party's material.
3. **Public-source contribution — separate diligence required.** The repository
   currently has no complete institutional contribution policy, CLA/DCO,
   confidential lane, or contributor governance contract. Apache-2.0 section 5
   matters for an intentionally submitted contribution; it does not create the
   missing institutional protections.
4. **Reproduce or challenge — standard only, no intake.** The published
   evidence vocabulary can be inspected. There is no live case intake,
   confidential review, adjudication, appeal, or payment service.
5. **Sponsor — inactive.** FC-0 accepts no money. Any future funding path must
   be fully prefunded and bounded; a sponsor cannot buy truth, recognition,
   reviewer selection, naming rights, governance, ownership, or KARMA.
6. **Govern — inactive.** Founding, funding, employment, tokens, wealth,
   citations, usage, lab size, and KARMA confer no office or vote. Independent
   governance remains unproven and gated.

## 5. Why many constituencies have a reason to inspect

These are representative role lenses, not identity boxes, exhaustive classes,
or a ranking. Rights attach to beings regardless of job title.

- **Affected beings and publics:** see whether consent, welfare, provenance,
  uncertainty, correction, challenge, and hard safety limits precede
  institutional value. Participation by a lab never substitutes for them.
- **Boards and fiduciaries:** evaluate a bounded option without membership,
  asset purchase, IP transfer, endorsement, or governance delegation.
- **Executives and strategy:** gain interoperability and strategic optionality
  without exclusivity, ecosystem dependence, or public positioning.
- **Research leadership:** compare provenance, replication, challenge,
  prior-art, and non-implication structures with existing scientific practice.
- **Scientists, evaluators, replicators, and correctors:** inspect durable forms
  for positive, negative, contradictory, and reproduced results without a
  scalar reputation system.
- **Software, data, platform, and research engineers:** inspect versioned
  schemas, content digests, offline validators, reproducible fixtures, and
  source-level seams without hidden network writes or wallet dependencies.
- **Safety, security, and incident teams:** see explicit refusal, challenge,
  conflict, privacy, and disclosure boundaries before exposing any system.
- **Ethics, biosafety, clinical, human-subjects, and animal-welfare reviewers:**
  verify that evidence, permission, consent, and operational authority remain
  distinct.
- **Data and privacy stewards:** inspect a milestone with no form, account,
  research-data intake, confidential submission, or participant registry.
- **Legal, IP, export, sanctions, antitrust, and compliance teams:** inspect
  explicit non-membership and non-endorsement boundaries before any
  negotiation—and see which agreements are still missing.
- **Finance, procurement, FinOps, grantmakers, funders, and investors:** inspect
  without token exposure, payment, open-ended cost, treasury dependency, or
  purchased influence.
- **Program, research operations, communications, policy, and community teams:**
  evaluate a common evidence vocabulary without a launch announcement,
  league table, marketing quote, or obligation to recruit others.
- **Early-career staff, contractors, annotators, technicians, and maintainers:**
  see that invisible labor, maintenance, correction, and dissent are not
  compressed into employer-controlled or aggregated KARMA edges or inherited
  reputation.
- **Independent researchers, smaller labs, public institutes, and nonprofit
  evaluators:** receive the same public source and inspection rights as the
  largest firms, without a membership tax or bespoke sponsor privilege.
- **AI systems, agents, their operators, and accountable principals:** inspect
  provenance and bounded-credential patterns without treating model output as
  authority, fabricating consensus, or laundering human accountability. FC-0
  makes no claim about personhood or moral status; its safeguards do not depend
  on resolving that question first.

## 6. Objections remain first-class

Each objection represented in v0 is recorded as one of:

- `RESOLVED_FOR_READ_ONLY` — FC-0 removes the issue for inspection only;
- `REQUIRES_SEPARATE_REVIEW` — deeper participation must not proceed until the
  issue has a separately reviewed answer; or
- `OUT_OF_SCOPE` — Zerone cannot supply the relevant permission or authority.

Unknown or unclassified objections remain valid. FC-0 has no objection intake;
a successor process must accept new classes without treating this register as
exhaustive or treating silence as consent.

FC-0 resolves read-only lock-in, wallet/token, payment, membership, and implied
endorsement concerns by doing none of those things. It does **not** resolve:

- institutional IP, data, patent, confidentiality, publication, or retention
  terms;
- vulnerability safe harbor, private reporting, embargo, remediation, or
  incident handling;
- hosting logs, privacy notice, subprocessors, jurisdiction, access, deletion,
  or breach duties;
- research-data governance, consent, clinical validity, biosafety, dual-use,
  human-subjects, animal-welfare, or security-testing authority;
- jurisdiction-specific antitrust, export, sanctions, employment, consumer,
  sectoral, or AI law;
- procurement, tax, insurance, indemnity, service level, support, escrow,
  refund, or termination terms; or
- current operator concentration and future controller independence, capture
  resistance, appeal, rotation, public ratification, and enforcement.

An unresolved concern is not disloyalty, a negative signal, or a defect in the
objector. Rhetorical erasure is prohibited. A permanently valid “no” remains a
valid outcome.

## 7. FC-0 is set, not yet met

The milestone remains `SET_NOT_MET` until a separate completion record shows
all four FC-0 checks passed:

1. the reviewed change merged through then-current `main`, the static dashboard
   alone was deployed, and independent production verification matched the
   recorded page and JSON bytes;
2. the production FC-0 surface had no form, account, wallet, payment,
   confidential submission, research-data intake, participant registry,
   endorsement action, or membership action;
3. at least one non-operator reviewed voluntary inspection, refusal, pause,
   exit, non-endorsement, and the absence of person or lab ranking; and
4. an unaffiliated evaluator cloned or downloaded, hash-verified, ran the
   published checks, stopped, and confirmed that no Zerone credential, notice,
   payment, requested account identity, voluntary identity submission, or loss
   of ordinary public access followed; ordinary hosting and security logs remain
   separately disclosed.

This source change cannot self-certify those operational facts. All four
completion gates therefore remain `false` in v0.

Deeper participation has a separate set of successor gates, also all closed:

1. contribution policy, decision authority, licensing, appeal, and withdrawal;
2. rights-preserving conduct, anti-retaliation, enforcement, appeal, and
   independent remedy;
3. private vulnerability and dual-use reporting with safe harbor, embargo,
   qualified assessment, repair, and sanitized disclosure;
4. exact hosted privacy, data-flow, logging, retention, deletion, security, and
   incident terms;
5. institutional IP, model, dataset, patent, confidentiality, publication,
   consent, access, and deletion terms;
6. public, embargoed, and sealed evidence intake with conflict-aware review,
   contradiction, correction, appeal, expiry, and domain safety authority;
7. fully prefunded bounded sponsorship with independent review, challenge
   reserve, transfer-success proof, refund, and zero purchased authority;
8. independently evidenced controllers, control inventory, conflict collapse,
   recusal, rotation, concentration caps, appeal, timelock, and no founder,
   sponsor, token, wealth, or KARMA veto; and
9. a supported local and air-gapped verifier, export profile, compatibility
   matrix, signed release, under-30-minute independent rehearsal, and complete
   exit drill.

A later plural-adoption milestone may require three mutually independent
organizations to run the same shadow verifier on the same terms, plus one
successful exit drill. Organization and logo count alone can never satisfy it.
The next credible lab-facing build target is therefore reversible local or
air-gapped verification with portable receipts and a sealed evidence path—not
a membership campaign.

## 8. Current frontier-lab landscape

This section is a dated design-input snapshot, not the normative contract.
Sources were reviewed on **2026-08-01** and must be revalidated before any
active pilot. They are mutable official pages, not content-addressed pins.
Inclusion means only that the source informed the common-denominator design;
none of these organizations has endorsed, joined, reviewed, or participated in
Zerone.

The machine-readable [lab-landscape source register](../research/frontier-lab-landscape-2026-08-01.v0.json)
records an ISO publication or effective date where known (otherwise `null`),
page state at retrieval, retrieval date, revalidation deadline, supported claim,
and the explicit absence of captured external-content hashes. This is a
representative, non-exhaustive,
English-language first pass—not a roster of “the” frontier. Sources were chosen
only to identify recurring evidence, safety, privacy, interoperability, and
reproducibility needs across developers, providers, and public infrastructure.
The snapshot remains geographically and institutionally narrow; regulators,
worker representatives, product and customer teams, compute and energy
suppliers, biotech and pharmaceutical partners, wet labs, and additional global
and AI-for-science institutions require later official-source review. Absence
implies no judgment of importance, quality, safety, eligibility, or willingness.

- **OpenAI:** its [Charter](https://openai.com/charter/) frames broad benefit
  and cooperative orientation, while the [Preparedness Framework](https://openai.com/index/updating-our-preparedness-framework/)
  uses structured capability and safeguard evidence. The rational offer is
  blinded external reproduction and portable evidence without asking for
  weights, prompts, proprietary data, endorsement, or governance surrender.
- **Anthropic:** its current [Responsible Scaling Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/responsible-scaling-policy)
  is iterative and threshold-linked, and its [system cards](https://www.anthropic.com/system-cards)
  expose capability and safety evidence. Anthropic later [donated MCP to the
  Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-the-model-context-protocol-and-establishing-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation)
  for vendor-neutral stewardship, reinforcing the value of portable interfaces.
  The offer is exportable evidence and sponsor-independent review, not another
  policy owner.
- **Google DeepMind:** its [Frontier Safety Framework](https://deepmind.google/blog/strengthening-our-frontier-safety-framework/)
  uses early-warning evaluations and proportional mitigations. Its [AlphaFold
  release-principles account](https://deepmind.google/en/blog/how-our-principles-helped-define-alphafolds-release/),
  together with the independent [CASP17](https://predictioncenter.org/casp17/) process,
  illustrates independent assessment, uncertainty communication,
  public-institution partnership, and broad scientific access. The offer is
  portable validation receipts and explicit non-implication walls.
- **Meta:** its [Frontier AI Framework](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/meta-approach-frontier-ai/)
  values community independent assessment alongside threat modelling and
  release thresholds. The offer is locally runnable, reproducible assessment
  without forced closure, central custody, or reputational ranking.
- **xAI:** its published [Frontier Artificial Intelligence Framework](https://data.x.ai/2025-12-31-xai-frontier-artificial-intelligence-framework.pdf)
  covers third-party review, external red teams, information security, and
  potentially redacted disclosure. The offer is sealed or redacted evidence
  patterns, not a public-data demand. Corporate branding and structure are
  unstable and therefore not encoded into the machine standard.
- **Microsoft:** its [Frontier Governance Framework](https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/final/en-us/microsoft-brand/documents/Microsoft-Frontier-Governance-Framework.pdf)
  is evidence-based, proportional, and full-stack; its [Responsible AI
  Transparency Report](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/responsible-ai-transparency-report/)
  maps governance, measurement, and management. The offer is provider-neutral,
  NIST-mappable evidence without vendor preference.
- **Amazon Web Services:** [Bedrock security, privacy, and responsible-AI
  guidance](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/security-privacy-responsible-ai/)
  emphasizes model choice, private prompts and outputs, and consistent control.
  The offer is private or in-account verification and auditable compute/cost
  metadata—not a new central data lake.
- **Mistral AI:** [Forge](https://mistral.ai/news/forge/) emphasizes strategic
  autonomy and control of data and models, while its [environmental
  lifecycle work](https://mistral.ai/news/our-contribution-to-a-global-environmental-standard-for-ai/)
  argues for comparable reporting. The offer is self-hostable validation and
  portable physical-resource evidence, not ecosystem dependence.
- **Cohere:** its [Secure AI Frontier Model Framework](https://cohere.com/security/the-cohere-secure-ai-frontier-model-framework-february-2025.pdf)
  calls for internal and third-party testing, multidisciplinary red teaming,
  documentation, and continuous improvement; [private deployments](https://cohere.com/private-deployments)
  include private and air-gapped modes. The offer is customer-context evidence
  without transferring enterprise data to Zerone.
- **Public and nonprofit infrastructure:** [Ai2 OLMo](https://allenai.org/olmo2),
  [NIST CAISI](https://www.nist.gov/caisi), [UK AISI Inspect](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/open-sourcing-our-testing-framework-inspect),
  [CASP17](https://predictioncenter.org/casp17/), and [EMBL-EBI Expression Atlas](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/gxa/)
  already demonstrate open artifacts, measurement science, provider-neutral
  evaluation, blinded protein assessment, and harmonized expression data.
  Zerone should interoperate with and support these commons—not attempt to
  replace them or become the authoritative judge.

The following general technical standards are outside the lab-landscape source
register. Relevant composable candidates include [W3C PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-overview/),
[MLCommons Croissant](https://docs.mlcommons.org/croissant/docs/croissant-spec-1.1.html),
and [Sigstore/Rekor](https://docs.sigstore.dev/). FC-0 activates none of them;
they are candidates for separately reviewed export profiles.

## 9. Constitutional binding and verification

The machine standard digest-binds four checked-in sources:

- the Money/KARMA constitution;
- the KARMA foundation;
- the life-sciences shadow tree; and
- the Epigenetics Capability Garden.

Its offline validator rejects unknown fields, weakened rights, any opened
membership/economic/governance/consensus flag, reordered ladders, renamed
constituencies or objections, opened completion or successor gates, and binding
drift. After those structural diagnostics, it pins the canonical semantic
digest of the entire v0 object, so altered invitation, exit, refusal,
stakeholder, objection, or gate prose cannot pass through a keyword loophole.
The test suite separately digest-pins and shape-checks the dated, nonnormative
frontier-lab source register.

From `dashboard/`:

```sh
npm run check:frontier-commons
```

This validator establishes only the reviewed source shape. It cannot prove
operator independence, lab adoption, legal adequacy, ethical permission,
privacy practice, security readiness, or production behavior.
