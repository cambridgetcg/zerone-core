## Relationship and precedence

[Frontier Commons FC-0](frontier-commons-participation-v0.md) is Zerone's
public, read-only invitation surface of record. FL-0 is a subordinate internal
receipt shadow: it stress-tests one possible evidence shape and preserves the
reasoning, objections, and proposed safeguards that would have to govern a
future receipt lane. It is not a second participation compact.

FL-0 neither replaces nor amends FC-0, extends its invitation, satisfies any
FC-0 completion gate, nor authorizes outreach. If wording conflicts, FC-0
governs the meaning and scope of the invitation; FL-0 governs only validation
of this receipt-profile draft and applies the tighter no-outreach and
zero-effect boundary. The machine profile binds the exact current FC-0 JSON by
SHA-256 so this relationship cannot silently float to different invitation
terms.

## 1. The milestone

The governing objective is:

> Remove every avoidable Zerone-imposed reason not to participate, disclose
> every remaining reason to wait or decline, and preserve refusal as a valid
> outcome.

The literal claim that there can be “no reason not to join” would defeat the
goal. A laboratory can have a valid legal, safety, confidentiality, fiduciary,
competition, capacity, or conscientious reason to decline. An individual can
decline even when their employer participates. A model or agent cannot be
treated as having consented merely because an operator enrolled a service.

Zerone should instead become the easiest evidence commons to approach because
it is the safest to leave. Participation is earned by Zerone binding itself:
narrow scope, separate consent, no token gate, no capture of intellectual
property, no implied endorsement, no person score, portable artifacts, honest
correction, and no punishment for silence, rest, refusal, or exit.

FL-0 deliberately does only four things beneath FC-0:

1. publishes a reviewable receipt-lane analysis and objection ledger;
2. defines an offline, portable `One Bounded Inconclusive Receipt` profile
   using in-toto Statement v1;
3. dogfoods that profile on its own machine document with an unsigned `INCONCLUSIVE`
   receipt; and
4. freezes every economic, membership, authority, qualification, governance,
   network-write, affiliation, endorsement, outreach, and deployment effect at
   `false`.

It does not contact a lab, display a lab logo, open enrollment, create a
partnership, accept confidential evidence, move value, register an adapter, or
change a running chain. The machine document sits in the dashboard's public
asset tree and may therefore be served as inert inspection material by a later
dashboard deployment. Availability for inspection is neither an invitation,
enrollment, participant record, affiliation, endorsement, nor activation.

## 2. Reasoning, one layer at a time

### 2.1 Being-first

Existing human rights and other legally protected interests do not depend on
participation. Under moral uncertainty, this profile's proposed safeguards also apply a
precautionary procedural floor to artificial systems: bounded delegation,
controller provenance, stop paths, and no punitive recognition. That floor
does not assert consciousness, moral or legal personhood, legal rights,
consent capacity, or liability for an artificial system. FL-0 does not transfer
or allocate legal accountability to an artificial system; applicable law
remains controlling.

Organizations, teams, humans, and artificial systems are not interchangeable
units, and a corporate act cannot erase the agency of the beings inside it.
Any affected being or role omitted from the profile's taxonomy receives the
universal floor without forced classification; a category can add context but
cannot remove protection or manufacture consent.

Zerone requires no shared metaphysical conclusion about AI consciousness or
personhood. Under moral uncertainty, it should choose reversible actions,
bounded delegation, stop paths, and non-punitive treatment without reallocating
legal accountability; applicable law determines it. Uncertain ontology is
neither a license to dominate an artificial system nor a device for
transferring human responsibility to it.

Participation consequence: no human must prove worth, alignment, loyalty,
identity, or usefulness to inspect or use a public standard, and artificial-
system delegation must remain bounded, attributable, and stoppable. Refusal
consequence: no loss of existing human rights, unrelated public access, or
recognition of past work; an artificial-system “refusal” means a technical
stop, denied delegation, or controller-enforced boundary, not a claim of legal
consent capacity.

### 2.2 Epistemic

A signature can authenticate who asserted something and preserve its bytes. It
cannot make the assertion true. A chain can witness sequence and make quiet
rewriting detectable within its preserved history. It cannot certify ultimate
truth, safety, compliance, independence, or benevolence.

The useful unit is therefore a bounded relation among artifacts:

```text
receipt -- REPLICATES / CHALLENGES / DIVERGES_FROM --> receipt
```

These FL-0 relations are unauthenticated assertions with no automatic
correction, supersession, withdrawal, or reliance effect. `INCONCLUSIVE` and
`DIVERGED` are useful outcomes. Because v0 has no authenticated issuer profile,
it cannot prove who is refusing or that a refusal is voluntary; `REFUSED` and
self-refusal receipts are therefore reserved for a future authenticated
profile. Nobody may publish another party's silence, nonresponse, capacity, or
refusal as a receipt about them. The absence of a refusal receipt does not
weaken the right to decline. Absent expected evidence may be reported only
inside an already-authorized evaluation. Nonparticipation and silence are not
evidence and create no negative record, KARMA, or mark against a person.

### 2.3 Scientific

Frontier evaluation is meaningful only under an exact subject, method, threat
model, fixture, acceptance policy, environment, evidence cutoff, and stated
limitations. A favourable scalar without those bounds is not portable
evidence. Reviewer compensation, if ever introduced under a separate
agreement, must depend on faithful execution rather than a preferred verdict.

Public openness is not always safe. Already-public, low-risk evidence may use
the open lane. Trade secrets, personal reports, embargoed work, model weights,
private prompts, exploit instructions, secrets, and controlled information
stay outside Zerone. A digest of a low-entropy secret is not concealment;
private commitments require appropriate random blinding and a separately
reviewed confidential system. FL-0 implements no such system.

### 2.4 Commons

The commons should reduce duplicated assurance work by making authorized
public evidence portable across safety, security, research, procurement,
legal, product, and public-interest review. It should compose with existing
formats rather than require a laboratory to replace its own framework or make
Zerone authoritative.

The open core remains nonexclusive and forkable. Each artifact keeps its own
license and ownership. “Open source,” “open weight,” “source available,” and
“publicly described” are distinct; participation cannot silently normalize
their licenses.

### 2.5 Institutional

A laboratory is a collection of legal entities, controllers, business units,
teams, workers, contractors, services, models, and users. Participation must
name which one is speaking and for what artifact. A subsidiary cannot silently
bind its parent. An employee cannot silently bind an employer. Employer consent
cannot substitute for an individual's personal attribution or disclosure
consent.

Many addresses, signatures, employees, subsidiaries, model instances, or
agents under materially shared effective decision authority for the assessed
claim remain one control root for that independence analysis. Common vendors,
funders, datasets, employment, or infrastructure are disclosed factors, not
automatic identity collapse. This analysis affects claim-specific quorum
accounting; it does not erase legal, organizational, or personal identity.

### 2.6 Corporate

Boards and executives need bounded cost, measurable utility, non-implication
language, and no critical dependency. Legal and compliance need a data map,
license clarity, retention semantics, and jurisdictional review. Safety and
security need threat-scoped evaluation plus disclosure lanes that do not leak
hazards. Investors need zero token exposure and no acquisition or treasury
relationship. Competition counsel need a firewall around pricing, customers,
hiring, unreleased roadmaps, market allocation, private strategy, and secret
capabilities.

FL-0 provides no consideration and creates no protocol economic effect. That
does not make participation costless: time, compute, legal and security review,
and opportunity cost may remain. The profile analysis describes a narrow public-evidence
lane. It is not legal advice, a compliance mechanism, a privilege-preserving
repository, an enterprise SLA, or a confidential disclosure service.

### 2.7 Team

Research, safety, product, infrastructure, open-source, data, and people teams
derive different value from the same receipt. The common benefit is a reusable
answer to: what exact artifact was assessed, under what bounded method, by
which declared control root, with what result and limitations, and what later
challenged, diverged from, or replicated it?

Credit stays attached to the artifact and role. It never becomes a universal
person score, employee metric, compensation multiplier, access gate, rank, or
governance weight. A team may use a project identity so no worker must expose a
wallet or public personal identity.

### 2.8 Individual being

Humans retain privacy, attribution choice, refusal, appeal, correction,
anti-retaliation, and the ability to report through legally protected channels
outside Zerone. Contractors retain ordinary contract, wage, confidentiality,
and IP protections. No receipt changes employment or worker classification.

Models and agents receive only bounded, revocable authority with explicit
controller provenance, budgets, tool limits, and a stop path. These are
precautionary procedural safeguards, distinct from existing human and legal
rights. FL-0 does not claim artificial-system consciousness, sentience, legal
personhood, consent capacity, debt, liability, employment status, or voting
status. FL-0 records no model or agent consent and infers none from output,
compliance, refusal, error, technical stop, denied delegation, or shutdown;
none of those events creates negative recognition.

## 3. The proposed constitutional floor

These are proposed participation terms, not operational facts. The source
validator checks exact draft structure and closed effects; it does not validate
the promises legally, ethically, institutionally, or operationally.
Participant remedy, independent adjudication, and structural enforcement do
not yet exist. Every promise constrains Zerone source maintainers and only a
future receipt-profile steward that explicitly adopts it. It does not bind unrelated
third parties or guarantee their conduct. An observed breach stops the affected
Zerone lane and permits exit, but no remedy may be claimed before one is
implemented; applicable law controls.

1. **Voluntary scope.** Every lane is separately chosen; no bundle or funnel.
2. **Unbundled consent.** Participation, publication, identity disclosure,
   training use, publicity, governance, and economics are separate decisions.
3. **No exclusivity or minimum.** Inspection is a complete standalone access
   mode. Reading or local verification is not enrollment or participant status,
   creates no participation record, and may not be represented as participation
   without a separate opt-in. There is no minimum cadence, duration, artifact
   count, or reciprocal deal.
4. **Refusal, rest, inactivity, and exit.** None creates a penalty, negative
   KARMA, unrelated access loss, or adverse person inference.
5. **No financial gate.** No wallet, token purchase, stake, reward, treasury
   relationship, or financial promotion is required by FL-0.
6. **Data minimization.** No model weights, datasets, private prompts, personal
   data, secrets, confidential information, or controlled material is required.
7. **Participant ownership.** No default transfer of IP, data, models, or
   research; every license is explicit and artifact-scoped.
8. **No implied endorsement.** Participation cannot be represented as
   partnership, affiliation, certification, compliance, or approval.
9. **Attribution without rank.** Artifact and role attribution cannot become a
   global reputation, employment score, access score, or person-level KARMA.
10. **Correction and challenge.** Contradiction, minority reports,
    supersession, appeal, and withdrawal of future reliance remain first-class
    rights, but FL-0 implements only assertion-only challenge, divergence, and
    replication links with no automatic effect.
11. **Corporate/individual separation.** Neither side silently binds the other.
12. **Entity-scope non-imputation.** Participation attaches only to the exact
    named entity, team, role, and artifact; it does not imply participation by a
    parent, subsidiary, affiliate, workforce, customer, or investor.
13. **Controller collapse.** Nominal identities under materially shared
    effective decision authority for the assessed claim do not create an
    independent quorum. Shared vendors, funding, data, or infrastructure are
    factors to disclose, not automatic collapse.
14. **No retroactive burden.** Material scope changes require a new version,
    notice, an exit window, and renewed consent where necessary.
15. **Competition firewall.** No competitively sensitive coordination belongs
    in the commons.
16. **Safety disclosure lanes.** Unsafe public disclosure is refused; FL-0 has
    no confidential lane. A publisher must have lawful authority over its own
    public material, but permission from the challenged issuer or an employer is
    not required where law protects reporting. Zerone is not itself a protected
    reporting channel.
17. **Moral uncertainty.** No mandatory personhood doctrine; precaution and
    reversible treatment increase with plausible welfare interests and stakes.
18. **No model/agent assent inference.** FL-0 records no model or agent consent
    and infers none from output, compliance, refusal, error, technical stop,
    denied delegation, or shutdown.
19. **Fork and export.** Receipts verify without Zerone and may be mirrored;
    Zerone must not become a release-path dependency.
20. **Bounded emergency power.** A later steward's stop-publication power must
    be narrow, logged, appealable, independently reviewed, and self-expiring.

## 7. One Bounded Inconclusive Receipt profile

FL-0 uses an [in-toto Statement
v1](https://github.com/in-toto/attestation/blob/main/spec/v1/statement.md)
whose single subject has an exact SHA-256 digest. The predicate type is this
versioned specification and the predicate also carries its exact source digest,
so a mutable `main` URL cannot silently change the reviewed semantics. The
statement is independently portable and can be wrapped in DSSE and signed
through a separately pinned Sigstore policy. A valid outer signature would
authenticate the signer and bytes; it would not establish predicate truth,
organizational authority, independence, safety, or compliance.

The v0 predicate records:

- one bounded ASCII artifact identifier and exact digest. The canonical
  dogfood subject is verified against the profile bytes. A public receipt must
  supply the exact non-empty subject bytes, bounded to 1 MiB, and the validator
  recomputes their digest. This verifies byte identity only: the result remains
  `subjectSemanticsVerified: false`, and a larger subject requires a separately
  reviewed, bounded manifest profile;
- a claimed issuer and claimed control root, limited to artifact-only scope,
  plus explicit `authenticated: false`,
  `authorizedToRepresentOrganization: false`, and
  `systemAssentClaimed: false` in this unsigned version;
- creation, evidence-cutoff, review-after, and expiry dates;
- seven evaluation-material digest fields. An internal dogfood receipt must
  set all seven to `null`. A public evaluation must supply the exact underlying
  protocol, threat-model, fixture, acceptance-policy, environment, evidence,
  and challenge-policy bytes to the offline verifier, which recomputes every
  digest; a hash-shaped value without those bytes fails closed;
- one bounded claimed result: `RECORDED`, `REPRODUCED`, `DIVERGED`, or
  `INCONCLUSIVE`. The validator proves only that the declaration has an allowed
  shape; it does not prove the result true. `REFUSED` is reserved until a later
  profile can authenticate the issuer, scope, and voluntary authorization;
- bounded uppercase reason and limitation codes, with no arbitrary free text.
  Codes can still encode sensitive information and are not a data-loss-
  prevention mechanism. Reserving the formal `REFUSED` result also cannot stop
  a code or subject name from semantically alleging another party's refusal or
  silence. The validator therefore returns `claimSemanticsVerified: false`;
  publisher review must enforce the normative ban on third-party refusal
  claims;
- assertion-only `CHALLENGES`, `DIVERGES_FROM`, or `REPLICATES` relations.
  `DIVERGED` requires `DIVERGES_FROM`, `REPRODUCED` requires `REPLICATES`, and
  contradictory result/relation pairs fail;
- `PUBLIC_METADATA_ONLY` receipt-content classification, explicitly declared
  (not parser-proven) to contain no confidential data, personal data, model
  weights, training data, private prompts, exploit detail, secrets, or
  export-controlled material. Human data-owner classification remains required
  before publication;
- explicit false truth, safety, compliance, certification, endorsement,
  membership, economic, reward, KARMA, qualification, authority, governance,
  privacy-rights, and network-write effects; and
- correction semantics: this unsigned profile is assertion-only, creates no
  automatic correction or withdrawal, cannot delete public history, and cannot
  affect unrelated access. A future effectful graph requires referenced bytes,
  same-subject checks, chronology, authenticated authorization, and independent
  policy review; and
- an explicit caller-supplied validation date for any public evaluation. The
  validator echoes that date as `freshnessEvaluatedOn`; a relying party must
  supply its own trusted clock. A future-created or expired receipt fails
  currentness policy, while even a current receipt remains unauthenticated and
  ineligible for automatic reliance.

The dogfood receipt points to the exact profile JSON and says `INCONCLUSIVE`,
for four reasons: no bound evaluation materials, no external review, no
signatories, and no operational enforcement. Its seven evaluation-material
digest fields are intentionally `null`; it claims no hidden evidence. It is an
unsigned self-assessment, not frontier-lab participation or evidence that FL-0
is institutionally ready. Its limitations are bounded public codes rather than
free text. “Bounded” describes the disclosed scope and limits; the format cannot
prove honesty, truth, authority, privacy classification, or independence.
Its canonical raw bytes are pinned automatically whenever the parser sees the
`ZERONE_SELF_DOGFOOD` kind. A dynamic public receipt is not canonical; its
caller may supply `expectedReceiptSha256` to pin the exact receipt bytes in
addition to the mandatory subject and evaluation-material byte bindings.

### M0.1 declared-control-separated roundtrip — not run

M0.1 means a reviewer who did not author the assessed subject, this profile, or
the validator runs the exact canonical bundle on a clean machine, reproduces
the profile and receipt digests, exercises at least one fail-closed mutation,
and completes the local-copy exit rehearsal. The reviewer must disclose
conflicts and declare a claim-specific effective control root distinct from the
issuer's declared root. That is evidence of a review process, not proof of
independence: v0 does not authenticate either root or verify control
separation. No reviewer has completed M0.1 yet.

## 8. Corporate M1 — `NOT_READY`

FL-0 authorizes no institutional invitation, contract, reliance, confidential
exchange, publicity, or production dependency. Before asking any laboratory to
participate, every machine-listed M1 gate must close through separately
reviewed evidence. At minimum Zerone needs:

1. accessibility, labor, worker-classification, and whistleblower review;
2. an authenticated relation graph with same-subject, chronology, correction-
   authority, and referenced-byte checks;
3. a code of conduct with enforcement, appeal, anti-retaliation, and protected
   reporting paths;
4. competition and confidentiality review for the exact proposed scope;
5. contribution, IP, patent, publication, and license terms;
6. counterparty scope and signatory authority;
7. an explicit, accountable human decision authorizing the bounded outreach;
8. governing terms, governing law, jurisdiction, and a dispute process;
9. explicit authorized human data-owner publication classification;
10. independent governance, capture, custody, and remedy review;
11. independent review of the receipt parser, threat model, and material-
    binding behavior;
12. liability, warranty, indemnity, insurance, and participant-remedy terms;
13. logo, name, affiliation, endorsement, and publicity rules;
14. a completed M0.1 declared-control-separated non-author roundtrip;
15. maintainer, change-control, versioning, and deprecation rules;
16. a non-targeting outreach policy: no profiling or identity targeting,
    minimized lawful contact sources, at most one bounded contact, stop on
    silence or decline, and finite contact-data retention;
17. a privacy data map, DPA decision, retention, erasure, and public-permanence
    review;
18. procurement, tax, accounting, sanctions, export-control, and financial-
    promotion review;
19. security policy, coordinated disclosure, safe harbor, incident response,
    and embargo rules; and
20. service-level, support, availability, portability, and exit terms.

The external target, which is not FL-0's current state, is one authorized
frontier-lab team, one evaluator meeting the M0.1 non-author and declared-
control-separation test, one already-public low-risk artifact, and origin plus
reproduction/challenge receipts. The evaluator's independence remains
unverified until a later authenticated policy can establish it. Success is not
a favourable verdict or a logo. It is a useful, portable, challengeable receipt
that both parties can verify offline and leave behind without making Zerone a
dependency.

Acceptance targets for that later pilot are:

- at most one engineer-day of participant integration;
- clean-machine offline verification in under 60 seconds;
- independently configured signer policies;
- fail-closed mutation, replay, wrong-signer, wrong-subject, wrong-predicate,
  duplicate-key, and stale-policy tests;
- no confidential or personal data, weights, private prompts, or exploit detail;
- no ZRN, wallet, stake, reward, consideration, governance, qualification,
  protocol economic effect, or production dependency, while real time,
  compute, legal-review, and opportunity costs stay disclosed and capped;
- reuse by at least three participant functions; and
- successful assertion-only challenge-link and exit rehearsals; any effectful
  correction or supersession requires a later authenticated profile and review.

Stop rather than solve around the participant if the publisher's authorized
data, legal, or security classifier refuses release of nonpublic material, exit
is not clean, production credentials or model access become necessary, the
receipt implies truth/compliance/endorsement, a person becomes ranked or
surveilled, Zerone enters the release critical path, or cost exceeds the agreed
cap without renewed consent. This publication stop does not grant a challenged
issuer or employer a special veto over independently lawful reporting;
protected reporting remains outside Zerone.

## 10. Release and validation boundary

The JSON is a static schema-checked internal draft. Every release flag is false, and both
`actualParticipants` and `signatories` are empty. Its source validator rejects
unknown or reordered fields, unsafe paths or links, changed dependency digests,
opened effects, reserved seats/shares/vetoes, mandatory identity/data/token
requirements, weakened proposed protections, missing objections or honest
limits, claims of verified independence, unbound public-evaluation digests, semantic
profile drift, unsigned organizational authority, stale receipts, contradictory
relations, arbitrary receipt free text, or receipt drift. This validation does
not establish legal validity, institutional
acceptance, ethical adequacy, truth, safety, or operational enforcement.

The raw-string entry points enforce byte and nesting limits before JSON parse.
The JavaScript object entry points first serialize one inert snapshot to prevent
accessor/Proxy time-of-check/time-of-use changes, so they are for trusted local
callers only: serialization itself precedes the byte check and is not a safe
denial-of-service boundary for attacker-supplied objects or Proxies. Any future
service must accept bounded raw bytes or add an independently reviewed bounded
object traversal before exposing this validator.
